How Petey's microphone works.
Petey only acts on what you say to it. But to hear "Hey Petey", your browser keeps the microphone open and sends the audio to its speech service (to Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge, Apple in Safari, as far as we know) so it can turn speech into text. With "Tap and talk" the microphone is open only while you are speaking to Petey. Check your company's policy on talking near sensitive information.
